Goes with any side: air-fried vegetables, a salad, rice or cauliflower rice.

## Method
1. Pat the chicken very dry. Rub with oil, then the salt and spices.
2. Air fry at 380°F skin side down for 10 min.
3. Flip and cook 10–14 min more, until the skin is crisp and the thickest piece reads 175°F.
4. Rest 5 min before serving.

## Make it yours
- Swap the rub: chili powder and cumin for taco night, Italian seasoning with parmesan, or lemon pepper.
- Toss broccoli or green beans in oil and add them for the last 8 min.
