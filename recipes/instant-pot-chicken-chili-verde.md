Serve in bowls or keto tortillas, with rice on the side for anyone who wants it.

## Method
1. Put the stock, onion and garlic in the pot. Add the chicken, sprinkle with cumin, oregano and salt, and pour the salsa verde over the top.
2. Seal; High Pressure 10 min. Natural release 10 min, then quick release.
3. Shred the chicken in the pot with two forks and stir it into the sauce.
4. Serve with cilantro, lime and sour cream.

## Make it yours
- Stir in a can of drained green chiles or a diced jalapeño for more heat.
- Leftovers make good quesadillas on keto tortillas.
