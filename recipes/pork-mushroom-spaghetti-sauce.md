Serve over spaghetti for most guests, or zoodles (or other veg noodles) for keto and low-carb guests.

## Method
1. Dice the onion and garlic.
2. Brown the pork with the onion and garlic (or mirepoix).
3. Add the tomato paste, then the mushrooms.
4. Add lots of Italian seasoning.
5. Add the crushed tomatoes and 1 can of water (measure with a tomato can).
6. Bring to a boil, then simmer 30–90 minutes.
7. Serve over spaghetti or zoodles.

## Notes
- Closer to 30 minutes is fine for a quick dinner; closer to 90 gives a deeper, thicker sauce.
