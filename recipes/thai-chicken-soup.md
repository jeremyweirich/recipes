Serve with crusty bread or white rice for non-keto guests.

## Method
1. Mince the ginger and garlic and sauté over medium heat.
2. Add the chicken and brown it, then add everything except the lime.
3. Bring to a boil and simmer for 30 min.
4. Squeeze in the lime right before serving.

## Notes
- Keto: the carrots add only about 3 g net carbs per serving across 4 bowls, so they can stay. To cut them, use zucchini or daikon instead.
