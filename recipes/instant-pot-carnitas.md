Serve in keto tortillas or lettuce cups, with rice and beans on the side for anyone who wants them.

## Method
1. Toss the pork with salt, cumin, oregano and chili powder.
2. Put the stock, lime juice, onion and garlic in the pot, then add the pork.
3. Seal; High Pressure 35 min. Natural release 15 min, then quick release.
4. Shred the pork, discarding the onion. Toss with a few spoonfuls of the cooking liquid.
5. Air fry at 400°F in a single layer for 5–8 min, until the edges are crisp. Work in batches.

## Make it yours
- Freeze shredded pork in portions before crisping; crisp straight from thawed.
- Use it for burrito bowls, nachos on pork rinds, or eggs in the morning.
