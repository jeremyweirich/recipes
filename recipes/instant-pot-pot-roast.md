Radishes stand in for potatoes and taste milder once cooked. Mashed potatoes or rice on the side work for anyone not eating keto.

## Method
1. Cut the roast into 3 chunks and season with salt and pepper.
2. Sauté high: brown the chunks in oil, about 3 min per side. Set aside.
3. Add the broth and scrape the bottom clean. Add the Worcestershire, thyme, bay leaves, onion and garlic, then the beef. Turn Sauté off.
4. Seal; High Pressure 60 min. Natural release 15 min, then quick release.
5. Add the radishes and celery around the beef. Seal; High Pressure 4 min, quick release.
6. Remove the bay leaves. For gravy, simmer the liquid on Sauté for 10 min or whisk in xanthan gum.

## Make it yours
- Add 2 carrots with the radishes if you're not strict about carbs.
- Shred leftovers for sandwiches or tacos.
