Tastes like proper buttery mashed potatoes, and nobody at the table needs to be keto to want seconds. About 7 g net carbs per serving (rough estimate). Great under the pot roast or either pork stew.

The one thing that matters: get the cauliflower **dry**. Water is what makes cauliflower mash soupy, so steam it rather than boiling it, and let it steam off before blending.

## Method
1. Steam the cauliflower and garlic until a fork slides through with no resistance, 12–15 min. (Instant Pot: 1 cup water, trivet, florets and garlic on top; High Pressure 3 min, quick release.)
2. Drain well, spread on a sheet pan or leave in the colander, and let it steam dry for 5 min. For the creamiest result, press it in a clean kitchen towel to squeeze out more water.
3. Put the hot cauliflower and garlic in a food processor with the butter, cream cheese, half the cream, the parmesan, salt and pepper. Blend until completely smooth, about 1 min, scraping down once.
4. Add more cream a spoonful at a time until it's as loose as you like. Taste and adjust salt.
5. Serve topped with a pat of butter, more pepper and the chives.

## Notes
- No food processor: an immersion blender works in the pot, or use a potato masher for a rustic, chunkier mash.
- Make ahead: refrigerate up to 3 days. Reheat covered at 350°F for 20 min, or in the microwave, stirring halfway.
- Loaded version: fold in shredded cheddar, crumbled bacon and sliced green onions, then bake at 400°F until bubbly, about 15 min.
