For anyone not eating keto, spoon in some cooked rice to make it taste like classic cabbage rolls.

## Method
1. Sauté: brown the beef with the onion and garlic (~7 min). Drain extra fat if there's a lot.
2. Add the broth and scrape the bottom clean. Stir in the cabbage, diced tomatoes, Worcestershire and seasonings. Pour the tomato sauce on top. Turn Sauté off.
3. Seal; High Pressure 5 min. Natural release 5 min, then quick release.
4. Taste for salt and serve with sour cream.

## Make it yours
- Use ground pork or half pork, half beef.
- A splash of cider vinegar at the end gives it a sweet-and-sour cabbage roll taste.
