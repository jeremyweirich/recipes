Eat it as is, or in keto tortillas or hoagie rolls for anyone not eating keto.

## Method
1. Toss the peppers and onion with oil, Italian seasoning and salt.
2. Put the vegetables in the basket and the sausages on top.
3. Air fry at 380°F for 15–18 min, shaking halfway, until the sausages read 160°F.

## Make it yours
- Use chicken sausage, or hot Italian sausage if you like heat.
- Top with melted provolone, or spoon over warm marinara.
