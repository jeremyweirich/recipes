The fastest dinner here. Rice or a salad on the side.

## Method
1. Pat the salmon dry and season with salt and pepper. Mix the butter and garlic and brush it over the tops.
2. Air fry at 400°F skin side down for 7–9 min, until it flakes easily.
3. Squeeze lemon over the top and serve.

## Make it yours
- Swap the garlic butter for pesto, everything bagel seasoning, or a mix of mayo and Dijon.
- Add asparagus to the basket; it cooks in the same time.
