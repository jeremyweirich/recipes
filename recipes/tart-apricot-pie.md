A sunny, tangy pie that tastes like the best part of apricot jam. Dried apricots make it work any time of year and give a deeper, tarter flavor than most fresh apricots you can buy. Serve with vanilla ice cream or lightly sweetened whipped cream to play off the tartness.

You'll also need about 2½ cups water. Bake on a sheet pan: the filling likes to bubble over.

## Method
1. **Cook the apricots.** Put the apricots and 2½ cups water in a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil, cover, and simmer on low until very soft, 20–25 min. About ¾ cup of liquid should be left; add a splash of water if it's dry.
2. **Thicken.** Whisk the sugar, cornstarch and salt together, then stir into the apricots. Cook, stirring, until thick and glossy, about 2 min. Lightly mash about a third of the apricots with the spoon so the filling holds together.
3. **Finish the filling.** Off the heat, stir in the lemon zest and juice, butter and almond extract. Taste: it should be bright and tart. Let it cool to room temperature, about 30 min (or chill it up to 2 days).
4. **Heat the oven** to 425°F with a rack in the lower third and a sheet pan on it.
5. **Assemble.** Fit one crust into a 9-inch pie plate. Spread in the cooled filling. Cut the second crust into 1-inch strips and weave a lattice on top. Trim, fold the edge under and crimp. Brush the lattice and edge with egg wash and sprinkle with turbinado sugar. Chill 15 min while the oven finishes heating.
6. **Bake** on the hot sheet pan at 425°F for 20 min, then turn down to 375°F and bake 30–35 min more, until the crust is deep golden and the filling bubbles thickly through the lattice. Cover the edge with foil if it browns too fast.
7. **Cool at least 3 hours** before slicing so the filling sets.

## Notes
- Fresh apricots (summer): use about 2½ lb, pitted and quartered (about 6 cups). Skip step 1; toss them raw with the sugar, salt, lemon, almond extract and ¼ cup cornstarch, dot with the butter after filling the crust, and bake the same way until it bubbles in the center, 55–65 min total.
- Even tarter: add the juice of a second lemon, or cut the sugar to ⅓ cup.
- Make ahead: bake up to a day ahead and keep loosely covered at room temperature. Rewarm slices at 350°F for 10 min if you like it warm.
