Serve over rice for non-keto guests, and on its own or over cauliflower rice for keto guests. About 9 g net carbs per serving without rice (rough estimate). Needs a 6 qt or larger Instant Pot.

## Method
1. Pat pork dry; season with salt and pepper.
2. Sauté high: sear pork in oil in two batches (~12 min). Set aside.
3. Sauté medium: cook onion and celery (~5 min); stir in garlic, tomato paste, paprika for 1 min.
4. Deglaze with wine, scraping the bottom fully clean (avoids the Burn warning); reduce by half. Add broth, Worcestershire, thyme, bay leaves; return pork. Turn Sauté off.
5. Seal; High Pressure 30 min. Natural release 10 min, then quick release.
6. Stir in turnips and mushrooms; High Pressure 3 min, quick release.
7. Skim fat, remove bay leaves. Sauté to reduce gravy ~10 min, or whisk in xanthan gum. Adjust salt.
8. Serve with parsley.

## Notes
- Broth is reduced because a sealed pot loses little liquid.
- 30 min = tender chunks; 40 min = shreddable. Don't pass the max fill line.
- Slow cooker: sear first, low 8 h, add turnips and mushrooms for the last 2 h.
