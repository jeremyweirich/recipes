Add rice or mashed potatoes on the side for anyone not eating keto.

## Method
1. Toss the sprouts with 1 tbsp oil and ½ tsp salt. Air fry at 380°F for 15 min, shaking halfway, until browned. Set aside.
2. Rub the chops with the rest of the oil, salt, pepper, paprika and garlic powder.
3. Air fry the chops at 400°F for 10–12 min, flipping halfway, until they read 145°F. Rest 3 min.
4. Return the sprouts to the basket for 2 min to reheat, and top with parmesan.

## Make it yours
- Drizzle the sprouts with a little balsamic or hot honey (not keto).
- Bone-in chops work too; add 2–3 min.
