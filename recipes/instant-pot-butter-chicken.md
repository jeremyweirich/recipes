Serve over cauliflower rice, or regular rice for anyone who wants it.

## Method
1. Pour the stock into the pot and add the chicken, garlic, ginger, spices and salt. Stir once.
2. Pour the tomato sauce over the top and don't stir it in (this prevents the Burn warning).
3. Seal; High Pressure 8 min. Natural release 10 min, then quick release.
4. Stir in the butter and cream. If you want it thicker, simmer on Sauté for 5 min.

## Make it yours
- Add a pinch of cayenne for heat, or more cream for a milder sauce.
- Stir in a few handfuls of spinach at the end; it wilts in a minute.
