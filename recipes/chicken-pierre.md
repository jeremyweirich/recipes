Serve over rice for non-keto guests, or cauliflower rice for keto guests.

## Method
1. Pat the chicken breasts dry, then season with salt and pepper.
2. Brown the chicken in butter, then add the stewed tomatoes and seasonings.
3. Bring to a boil, then simmer on low for 30 min.
4. Serve over rice or cauliflower rice.
