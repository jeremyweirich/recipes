Crusty bread on the side is good for anyone not eating keto.

## Method
1. Sauté: brown the sausage, breaking it up (~6 min). Add the onion and garlic and cook 3 min.
2. Add the stock and scrape the bottom clean. Add the cauliflower. Turn Sauté off.
3. Seal; High Pressure 2 min, quick release.
4. Stir in the kale and cream. Let it sit 3 min until the kale wilts. Season with salt and the chili flakes.
5. Serve with parmesan.

## Make it yours
- Add a few slices of chopped cooked bacon on top.
- For a non-keto version, use 1½ lb diced potatoes instead of cauliflower and pressure cook 5 min.
